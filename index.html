Questions before your first lesson

Why is the trial lesson paid?+

It costs $10 so we both arrive ready to work. You still get a full 45 minute lesson: a level check, a talk about your goals and a personal study plan.

Can I get my money back?+

Yes, for any lesson you have paid for but not taken yet. Completed lessons are not refunded, and package lessons are refunded at the package rate.

Can I cancel or reschedule?+

Yes. Give at least 24 hours notice and you get a full refund or a new time. Later cancellations are charged.

How do I pay?+

When you book, by Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, JCB or PayPal.

Can I buy several lessons at once?+

Yes. 5 lessons cost $135 ($27 each) and are valid for 2 months. 10 lessons cost $260 ($26 each) and are valid for 4 months. Lessons you haven't taken are refunded at the package rate.

What level do I need?+

Any. Every lesson is matched to your level, from complete beginner to advanced.

Do I need to speak English to start?+

No. I can explain everything in Russian, and I understand Ukrainian.

How often should I have lessons?+

At least once a week; two or three times is better. Plan about two hours of practice on your own for every hour with me.

When will I notice progress?+

Students who practise every day usually feel a difference within two to four weeks.

Do you prepare students for exams?+

Yes, IELTS and TOEFL. Tell me your exam date in the trial lesson and we build the plan around it.

What do I need for a lesson?+

A computer or phone, headphones and a quiet room. Zoom opens in your browser.

What if I'm running late?+

The lesson still ends at its scheduled time, so try to join a couple of minutes early.
